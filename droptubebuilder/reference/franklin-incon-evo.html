Containment Pump Request Ignored

[Engineering] The containment called for the STP to turn on but saw no increase in vacuum level. Either the pump did not run, or it ran and the vacuum did not build. Action: check whether the STP actually ran when requested, which splits this immediately into a pump problem or a vacuum path problem; check the STP control wiring and the relay; check the STP is not locked out by a tank or line leak alarm; if the pump ran, check the vacuum path for a large leak that prevents any vacuum building; check the vacuum line, fittings and the transducer connection; check the interstitial space is actually sealed, including sump penetrations and test ports left open after service; check the transducer is reading, since a dead transducer reports no increase even when vacuum is building

Franklin / INCON · EVO 550 / 5000

Failed to Hold Vacuum

[SCM] Vacuum is decaying faster than the learned rate. The containment is leaking somewhere, though not necessarily through the tank wall. Action: check the accessible fittings, test ports and vacuum line connections first, since these are the common leak points and are far more likely than a tank breach; check any port opened during recent service was properly resealed; check the vacuum line for damage and for crushing under a sump lid; check the transducer connection and its seal; check sump penetrations and boots on the interstitial path; if the accessible points are sound, the interstice itself is suspect, which makes this a potential breach requiring investigation; a confirmed containment breach is a suspected release the owner must report to the implementing agency within 24 hours

Franklin / INCON · EVO 550 / 5000

Failed to Reach Target Vacuum

[SCM] Six inches of mercury could not be reached in the learned time. The system is pulling but not getting there. Action: check for a leak in the vacuum path, working the accessible fittings, test ports and line connections first; check any service port left open or poorly resealed; check the vacuum pump or generator is running and making its rated performance; check the vacuum line for restriction, crushing or blockage; check the transducer is reading correctly against a known gauge, since a drifting transducer reports a target never reached; check whether the learned time is still appropriate, because a changed installation invalidates the learned parameters; if the path is sound and the pump performs, treat the interstice as suspect and investigate

Low Vacuum

[SCM] The vacuum level has dropped below one inch of mercury. At this level the containment is effectively unmonitored. Action: check the vacuum source is running; check the accessible fittings, test ports and vacuum line connections for leaks; check any port disturbed by recent service; check the vacuum line for damage or disconnection; check the transducer and its connection; check whether other containments on the same system are also low, which points at the vacuum source rather than one interstice; if no leak is found in the accessible path, treat the interstice as suspect and investigate, reporting within 24 hours if a breach is confirmed

Low Vacuum And Pump Request Ignored

[Engineering] Low vacuum together with a pump request that produced no response. Two related failures at once, which usually means one cause. Action: check whether the STP ran when requested, which separates a control fault from a vacuum path fault; check the STP control wiring, relay and any lockout from a tank or line alarm; check the vacuum path for a leak large enough to prevent vacuum building at all, starting at accessible fittings and test ports; check the vacuum line for disconnection or major damage; check the transducer is reading; treat a large unexplained leak as a potential containment breach and investigate rather than simply restarting the pump

Unstable Vacuum

[SCM] The vacuum level is fluctuating inconsistently with the learned parameters. An intermittent leak or a measurement problem. Action: check for an intermittent leak at fittings, test ports and the vacuum line, including anything that moves or flexes; check for a loose or partly seated cap or port cover, which is the classic cause of unstable rather than steady loss; check the transducer and its wiring for an intermittent connection; check for temperature driven fluctuation, since a shallow interstice can swing with ambient conditions; check whether the learned parameters were established under different conditions and need relearning; trend the readings rather than judging on a single sample
