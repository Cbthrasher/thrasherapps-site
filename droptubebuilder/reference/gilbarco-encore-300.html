Gilbarco Encore 300 Error Codes

Field reference for fuel system and underground storage tank technicians. 14 entries, taken from the DropTubeBuilder app.

Gilbarco · Encore 300

301

Communication with the display module has failed. Action: check the display harness and connector at both ends, since a loose connector is reported as a module failure far more often than the module has failed; check for water ingress behind the display; check the module has power; reseat the connector and power cycle; check the module's software version matches what the dispenser expects; note that Encore 300 parts are not interchangeable with the S series, so confirm the part before ordering

Gilbarco · Encore 300

302

The display module failed its self test. Action: power cycle and see whether it passes on a clean start; check the harness and connector for water and loose pins; check the module has a stable supply; check for corrosion or water damage on the module; swap with a known good module of the correct Encore 300 part to confirm, since S series parts will not work here

Gilbarco · Encore 300

303

Dispenser communication has timed out. Action: check the two wire or network cable and terminations at the junction box for water and corrosion, which causes most of these; check whether other dispensers are also affected, which separates a site fault from this one; check the site controller and interface board are powered; check the dispenser address matches what the controller expects; check for a duplicate address causing intermittent dropouts; check polarity if recent work disturbed the wiring

Gilbarco · Encore 300

304

A dispenser power fault. Action: check the breaker and incoming supply, measuring under load rather than at rest; check terminations inside the dispenser for looseness and corrosion; check the power supply output against specification; check whether the fault is site wide, which points upstream; verify configuration and calibration survived; if power faults repeat with no external cause, treat the power supply as failing

Gilbarco · Encore 300

305

A dispenser firmware mismatch. Components are running versions that do not agree, so behaviour is unpredictable. Action: check the version of each component against what the dispenser requires; check whether a recent board swap brought an incompatible version; load the correct versions and let each finish without interruption; verify configuration and calibration afterwards; confirm the correct Encore 300 software, since S series versions do not apply to this platform

Gilbarco · Encore 300

310

The payment module is unresponsive. Action: check the module has power; check its communication cable and connectors, particularly where the cable flexes at the door hinge; check whether other positions are affected, which points at the site system; power cycle the module and then the dispenser; check whether the payment network or site system was recently updated; check the module's software version

Gilbarco · Encore 300

311

Printer paper is low or empty. Action: reload with the correct paper roll, loaded the right way round; check the paper path is clear and clean; check the paper type is correct, since the wrong stock jams repeatedly; check the low paper sensor if it reports empty with paper fitted; keep spare rolls on site

Gilbarco · Encore 300

312

The card reader is malfunctioning. Action: check the reader cable and its connectors first, because the cable fails considerably more often than the reader; check the cable where it flexes at the door hinge; check for water in the connector; check the reader has power; clean the read path if cards are read intermittently; swap the reader only once the cable is proven, and confirm the correct Encore 300 part

Gilbarco · Encore 300

313

The pump handle was left up at startup. The dispenser will not authorise with a handle already lifted, which is a safety behaviour rather than a fault. Action: check whether a nozzle is genuinely left up, which is the usual explanation; return the handle to the hung position and power cycle; if no handle is up, check the hang up switch for sticking, damage or water; check the switch adjustment, since a maladjusted switch reads as lifted; check the switch harness for a short to ground; test the switch directly before replacing anything

Gilbarco · Encore 300

314

The pump is out of service due to a hardware fault. Action: read the surrounding event log entries, since they usually identify the actual subsystem rather than this general code; check which functions are unavailable, which localises the fault faster than testing everything; check power and all internal connections; check for water ingress and corrosion; power cycle and note what fails to come up; work the specific fault that emerges rather than this code on its own

Gilbarco · Encore 300

320

The pulser signal is missing. Action: check the pulser harness and connector at both ends, since the wiring fails more often than the pulser; check for water in the connector; check the pulser mounting and coupling for slipping or misalignment; check the pulser has power where the type requires it; check for a break in the harness where it flexes; swap in a known good pulser of the correct Encore 300 part to separate the pulser from the wiring

Gilbarco · Encore 300

321

Blender calibration has failed. Action: confirm both legs flow normally before attempting calibration again, since it cannot succeed on a starved leg; check the filters, shear valves and STP pressure on both legs; check the meters and pulsers on both legs; check the test measure and the procedure; repeat the calibration and prove each blended grade; Breaking a Weights and Measures seal to reach calibration is restricted to a registered service agent in most states, so confirm you are permitted before opening it, and arrange resealing and any required W&M notification afterwards

Gilbarco · Encore 300

322

A price per unit failure on a nozzle. Action: check which nozzle position is reporting; check the display harness and connector for that position; check the price for that grade has been downloaded and is correct; check display addressing for duplicates; check the POS is sending prices for this position; confirm the correct price displays before allowing sales on that nozzle

Gilbarco · Encore 300

323

Communication with the VaporVac module has been lost. Action: check the module has power and its indicators behave normally; check its harness and connectors, including anything disturbed by recent work; check for water ingress at the module; reseat the module and its connections; power cycle and see whether it enumerates; check what your local air district requires while vapour recovery is out of service, and record the outage
