Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Setup Data Warning

Configuration data was lost or changed (often after a battery/power issue). Action: review and re-enter System/In-Tank/Sensor setup; print a setup report and confirm against records.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Printer Out

Integrated printer is out of paper or the door is open. Action: reload paper roll, close door, clear the warning.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Printer Error

Printer mechanism fault or paper jam. Action: clear jam, reseat paper, cycle power to the console if it persists.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Battery Warning

Console backup battery is low; setup/data may not survive a power loss. Action: schedule battery replacement (board-mounted lithium).

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

AC Power Off

Console lost AC power and is running on backup or has shut down. Action: check breaker/UPS feeding the console; confirm dedicated circuit is on.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

EEPROM Failure

Non-volatile memory (setup storage) failed its self-test. Action: cycle power; if it returns, the CPU/memory board needs service.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

RAM Failure

Working memory failed its self-test. Action: cycle power; persistent fault means CPU board replacement.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Software Module Warning

A software/feature module mismatch or expired feature was detected. Action: verify installed features/licensing and firmware version.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Comm Alarm

Console lost communication with a device (probe, sensor, module, or remote display). Action: check the device wiring, module seating, and address/setup.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Auto Dial Failed

Scheduled modem/network report-out to the host failed. Action: verify phone line/Ethernet, host number/IP, and comm settings.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Maintenance Tracker

A scheduled maintenance interval has elapsed. Action: perform the due maintenance and reset the tracker.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Delivery Needed

Product level dropped below the delivery-needed limit. Action: schedule a fuel delivery; informational.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Low Product

Product volume is below the configured low limit. Action: order fuel; verify limit is set correctly for the site.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

High Product

Product volume is above the high limit (approaching capacity). Action: stop/observe deliveries; verify the high limit and tank chart.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Overfill Alarm

Tank exceeded the overfill limit (typically 90%) during/after a delivery. Action: stop delivery immediately; investigate delivery practices and overfill setpoint.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Max Product Alarm

Level reached the maximum (≈95%) limit. Action: cease filling; confirm overfill-prevention valve and setpoints.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

High Water Alarm

Water in the tank exceeds the high-water limit. Action: pump out water; inspect fill cap, spill bucket, and gaskets for intrusion; check for phase separation.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Water Warning

Water has reached the warning limit. Action: monitor; schedule water removal and find the source.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Low Temperature Warning

Product temperature is below the low limit (waxing/gel risk for diesel). Action: informational; monitor in cold weather.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Invalid Fuel Level Alarm

Probe is reporting an implausible level (floats stuck or product/water floats crossed). Action: pull and inspect the probe and floats for fouling or sticking.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Probe Out Alarm

Console is not seeing the probe. Action: check probe wiring/connections at the probe and module; verify probe address and canister.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Gross Test Fail

Static (gross 3.0 gph) leak test failed. Action: re-run when quiet/idle; if it fails again, investigate the tank for leaks.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Periodic Test Fail

Periodic (0.2 gph) leak test failed. Action: ensure adequate idle time and product level; investigate if it repeats.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Annual Test Fail

Annual (0.1 gph) precision test failed. Action: review test conditions; arrange tightness testing/leak investigation.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Periodic Test Needed

No passing periodic leak test within the required window. Action: ensure the tank gets quiet idle time to complete a test.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Annual Test Needed

No passing annual (0.1 gph) test within the window. Action: schedule conditions for a valid test.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Leak Alarm

Statistical leak detection declared a leak. Action: treat as a release indication; investigate per regulations.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Sudden Loss Alarm

An unexpected sudden drop in volume (not a sale/delivery) was detected. Action: check for theft, siphoning, or a leak.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Theft Alarm

Volume decreased without an authorized dispense. Action: investigate unauthorized removal.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Probe High Water (No Float)

Water exceeds limit but the water float is missing/stuck. Action: inspect probe water float assembly.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Fuel Alarm

A liquid sensor (sump/interstitial/dispenser pan) detected hydrocarbons. Action: locate and stop the leak; remove product from the containment.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Water Alarm

A liquid sensor detected water in containment. Action: remove water; find intrusion source (covers, seals, groundwater).

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

High Liquid Alarm

Liquid in containment reached the upper sensor threshold. Action: pump out and identify whether fuel or water; inspect for leaks.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Low Liquid Alarm

Liquid in an interstitial/brine sensor fell below limit (possible breach of double wall). Action: inspect interstitial integrity and brine level.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Liquid Warning

Liquid detected at the warning level. Action: investigate containment; monitor for escalation to alarm.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Water Out Alarm

A discriminating sensor lost its expected water reference. Action: inspect the sensor and its position.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Sensor Out Alarm

Console is not seeing the sensor (open/short or disconnected). Action: check sensor wiring and module; verify the sensor type/setup.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Short Alarm

Sensor wiring is shorted. Action: inspect for pinched/wet wiring and connector corrosion; megger if needed.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Sensor Setup Data Warning

Sensor configuration was lost/changed. Action: re-enter sensor type and thresholds.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Line Gross Test Fail

Pressurized line leak detector failed the gross (3.0 gph) test. Action: line is disabled; inspect piping/fittings before returning to service.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Line Periodic Test Fail

Line failed the monthly (0.2 gph) test. Action: investigate piping integrity; verify functional element/leak detector.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Line Annual Test Fail

Line failed the annual (0.1 gph) test. Action: schedule line tightness testing/repair.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Line Equipment Alarm

PLLD/WPLLD detected a hardware/handling fault (e.g., relay, pressure sensor). Action: inspect line leak module, transducer, and STP relay wiring.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Pump Sensor Alarm

The line pressure/turbine sensor reading is out of range. Action: check the pressure transducer and its wiring at the STP.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Vapor Pressure Sensor Out

Vapor/ullage pressure sensor not communicating. Action: check the pressure sensor and its module/wiring.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Gross Pressure Warning

Ullage pressure is outside the normal range (Stage II/ORVR/PV monitoring). Action: inspect P/V vents and vapor lines for blockage or leaks.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Vapor Sensor Fuel Alarm

A vapor-well sensor detected liquid hydrocarbons. Action: investigate vapor recovery piping and well.

Veeder-Root · TLS-450 PLUS

Vapor Processor Warning

Membrane/vapor processor reported a fault or degraded performance. Action: review processor diagnostics and maintenance.
