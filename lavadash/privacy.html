Lava Dash Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Lava Dash is a floor is lava party game made by ThrasherApps. There is no account and no login, and ThrasherApps runs no servers for the game. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information ThrasherApps Collects

None. ThrasherApps does not collect, store or receive any personal information from Lava Dash. Game settings, such as your chosen mode, difficulty, number of players and the sound, music and vibration switches, are stored on your device only.

Advertising

The free version of Lava Dash shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and how ads are viewed and tapped. Google handles this information under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Before any ads load, Lava Dash shows Google's consent form where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states). The app then shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Ads are small banners at the bottom of the screen. Lava Dash shows no full screen or video ads.

Remove Ads Purchase

Lava Dash offers one optional in app purchase, Remove Ads, a one time payment that turns off all ads. When ads are removed, the ads SDK no longer requests ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; ThrasherApps never receives your payment details.

Camera, Microphone and Location

Lava Dash does not use the camera, the microphone, your contacts, your photos or precise location.

Children

Lava Dash is a family game meant to be played together, with a grown up running the phone. ThrasherApps does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents who prefer no ads at all can use the Remove Ads purchase, or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track for the device.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the revised policy is posted here.
