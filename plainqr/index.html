What it does

Many QR apps charge $7 to $10 a week to read something your camera can almost read on its own. Plain QR is the honest one.

Checks every link

Before you open a link, Plain QR says in plain words where it goes and flags the usual scam tricks: look alike brand sites, number addresses, hidden short links, unencrypted pages and app downloads.

Reveals short links

Tap Check where it goes to see the real destination of a short link without opening the page.

Does the obvious thing

Join a Wi-Fi network, add a contact, call, write an email, open a map or add a calendar event, with one tap and never on its own.

Reads photos too

Scan a code from a screenshot or a photo you already took, as well as with the live camera. Reads QR codes and the common barcodes.

Makes codes

Create codes for links, Wi-Fi, contacts, email, phone numbers and messages, in your own colors, with a warning if the colors would scan badly.

Pay once, never weekly

Every feature is free for 3 days. After that, a single $4.99 payment unlocks Plain QR for good. Nothing is ever charged automatically, and it is never a subscription.
