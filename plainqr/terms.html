What Plain QR Does

Plain QR reads QR codes and barcodes, explains what they contain, and offers actions such as opening a link, joining a Wi-Fi network or adding a contact. Nothing happens until you tap an action.

Link Safety Notes

The link safety notes are automatic checks for common warning signs. They are a help, not a guarantee. A link with no warnings can still be harmful, and a link with a warning can be legitimate. Decide for yourself whether to trust a code, especially before entering passwords or payment details on any page it opens.
