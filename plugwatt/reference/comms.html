Control pilot signaling

The J1772 pilot is a 1 kHz square wave between plus 12 V and minus 12 V. Its DC level names the state; its duty cycle advertises available current. Every AC session and the front door of every DC session runs on it.

IEC 61851 charging modes

International mode language for how a vehicle takes power. North American equipment maps cleanly onto it, and European built chargers report faults in these terms.

The high level language on top of the pilot. Plug and Charge signs the session with certificates so authorization is automatic; vehicle to grid rides the same protocol.
