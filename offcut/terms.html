What Offcut Does

Offcut scans the videos in your photo library on your device and groups them into categories: screen recordings, duplicates, long clips, slow motion, edited videos, and the largest files. It helps you review those groups and remove the ones you choose.

You are always in control. Offcut never selects anything on your behalf. Nothing is removed without your selection, your confirmation on the review screen, and the iOS system confirmation that follows.

Deletion and Recovery

Videos you remove are moved by iOS to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where Apple keeps them recoverable for 30 days. After that period the Photos app removes them permanently. Offcut has no ability to delete anything permanently and no ability to recover anything after Photos has cleared it.

If iCloud Photos is enabled, a video you remove is removed from iCloud and from your other devices signed into the same Apple Account. If you use an iCloud Shared Photo Library, removing a shared item affects the other participants. This is how iCloud works and it is outside Offcut’s control.

Please review your selections carefully. You are responsible for what you choose to remove.

Detection Is a Suggestion, Not a Judgement

Offcut identifies duplicates by comparing file size, pixel dimensions and duration, and identifies screen recordings, slow motion, time lapse and edited videos from the information iOS reports about each item. These are aids to speed up your review. They are not guarantees, and two different videos can share the same size and duration.

Where Offcut groups copies together it suggests one to keep and does not allow that suggestion to be selected for removal, so a whole group cannot be removed in a single action. You should still confirm each selection yourself before removing anything.

Storage figures shown in the app are estimates based on what iOS reports. Actual space recovered depends on iOS, on iCloud, and on when the Recently Deleted album is emptied.
