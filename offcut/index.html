Offcut: Video Cleaner

Your videos are probably the reason your phone is full. Not your photos, not your apps. Offcut finds the screen recordings you never watched again, the same clip saved twice, and the long videos taking more room than anything else. Everything runs on your device, and nothing goes until you say so.

What it does

Every other cleaner shows you the biggest number it can find. This one shows you the real one.

⏺ Screen recordings

Made once for a reason you have long since forgotten, and never watched again. Usually the easiest storage anyone ever gets back.

⧉ Duplicates

The same video saved more than once. Offcut keeps one copy and marks the rest, and it will never let you select both.

⏱ Long clips

Videos over five minutes, which take more room than anything else on the device. Plus slow motion, edited clips, and your largest files.

📊 An honest number

Each video counted once, even when it appears in two lists, and the Largest list left out of the total entirely. What you see is what you would actually get back.

🛟 Nothing goes until you say so

Offcut suggests, you choose. Removals go to Recently Deleted in Photos and stay recoverable for thirty days. Offcut cannot delete anything permanently.

🔒 Nothing leaves the device

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no server. There is no code in the app that could send a video anywhere, because there is no networking code at all.

Screen recordings and duplicates are free, and stay free. Offcut Pro opens long clips, slow motion, edited videos and the largest list. If iCloud Photos is on, a video you remove leaves your other devices too.
