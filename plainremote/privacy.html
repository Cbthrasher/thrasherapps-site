Plain Remote Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Plain Remote is made by ThrasherApps (thrasherapps.com).

The short version

Plain Remote does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. ThrasherApps never receives anything from the app.

Your home network

To find and control your TV, Plain Remote talks directly to devices on the Wi-Fi network your iPhone is on, after you allow local network access. It sends button presses, typed search text and app launches to your TV, and reads back the TV's name, model and installed apps. None of this leaves your home network or passes through ThrasherApps.

What stays on your iPhone

The list of your TVs, their addresses and the pairing keys your TVs issue are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting a TV in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

Purchases

Plain Remote Pro is bought through Apple. ThrasherApps never sees your payment details.

Children

Plain Remote does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@thrasherapps.com.
