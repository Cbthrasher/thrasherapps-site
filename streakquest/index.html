What it does

📈 Streaks that motivate

Every completed day grows the streak. The longer it runs, the more it pulls you back tomorrow.

🧊 Streak freezes

Life happens. A freeze saves the run on the day everything goes sideways, so one bad day does not undo a month.

🗂️ Categories that fit

Fitness, learning, productivity, wellness. Build the set of habits you actually want, not a template.

🔒 Yours alone

Your habits stay on your device. No ads, no tracking.
