Frequently asked

How do I fill a form?

First tap the gear icon, then Tester info and signature, and enter your name, company, certification and test equipment, and draw your signature once. Then on the Forms tab pick the state and the test, enter the site and the readings, and tap Fill the official form (or Create the test report in a state without a form). The finished PDF opens with your signature on it, ready to send by Mail or Messages, save to Files, or save to Photos for the work order.

Which states have their official form built in?

31 states and DC, more than 130 agency forms. Every other state gets a complete test report with every reading, the pass or fail result and the rule it comes from. The UST Tester page lists each state's forms.

Does UST Tester need a signal?

No. The forms, the pass and fail rules, the procedures, the equipment listings and the calculators all ship inside the app, so it works in a sump or a basement. Sending a finished form waits until you have signal.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: every calculator, the NWGLDE equipment directory, each state's forms overview and the federal testing intervals. Pro fills the state forms and test reports, and unlocks the make and model procedures, the troubleshooting guides, the field notes and the item by item forms help. The monthly and annual plans start with a 1 week free trial.

What happens if I leave a test half done?

Every answer saves as you type. Close the app, take a call, come back tomorrow: the test is where you left it. Start a new test from the menu at the top right of the test screen.

How do sites and due dates work?

Each finished form files itself under its site on the Sites tab, with the next due date worked out from the federal interval, or your state's where it is stricter. The Sites tab lists what is overdue, failed or due in the next 30 days, by site or by owner, and the app reminds you before each test is due. Tests done before you had the app can be logged on the site page.

Can I send one packet for a whole visit?

Yes. Open the site and choose the day under Visit packets: a cover page with every result and next due date, then each form from that day, in one PDF.

I work in more than one state.

On the Forms tab, tap + States and pick every state you work. Each one gets a one tap chip, and a site's own state decides which form its tests fill.

Can I keep my own copies of forms?

Yes. On the Forms tab, add a PDF or a photo of a form from Files under My forms for that state. You can view, share, rename, move it to another state, or delete it.

Who decides pass or fail?

You do. The app works out the drop, the vacuum lost or the leak rate from your readings and shows the result the form's own criteria give, with the source. The state's current form and rules, 40 CFR 280 and the manufacturer's manual govern, and you sign the form.

A form, state, make or model I need is missing.

Email it to support@thrasherapps.com. When a state changes its form we update the app, and requests from working testers set the order.

How do I move to a new phone?

Your sites, forms and settings live on your phone and move with your iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) when you set up the new phone from it. Then open Settings in UST Tester and tap Restore purchases while signed into the same Apple account.

Is my data private?

Everything stays on your device. No account, no server, no analytics. See the privacy policy.
