Frequently asked

Does UST Tester need a signal?

No. Every procedure, guide, forms page, equipment listing and calculator ships inside the app, so it works in a sump or a basement.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: every calculator, the full NWGLDE equipment directory, the federal testing intervals and records guides, the new site test order, and each state's forms overview. Pro unlocks the make and model procedures (leak detectors, dispenser calibration, tank and pipe install tests), the troubleshooting guides, the tank and line field notes, and the item by item forms help.

I work in more than one state.

On the Forms tab, tap + States and pick every state you work. Each one gets a one tap chip, and you can switch between them on site.

Can I keep my own copies of forms?

Yes. On the Forms tab, add a PDF or a photo of a form from Files under My forms for that state. You can view, share, rename, move it to another state, or delete it. Your forms stay on your phone.

Where does the information come from?

Each page lists its sources at the bottom: the manufacturer's manual, the agency's own form or rule, the federal rule in 40 CFR 280, or the NWGLDE listing. Always follow the current manual and your state's current form if they differ.

A state, make or model I work with is missing.

The library grows with app updates and requests from working testers set the order. Email the state form, or the make and model, to support@thrasherapps.com.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Open Settings in UST Tester and tap Restore purchases while signed into the same Apple account.

Is my data private?

Everything stays on your device. No account, no server, no analytics. See the privacy policy.
