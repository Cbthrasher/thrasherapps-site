UST Tester Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

UST Tester is a field reference for underground storage tank testers. It is built to work in a sump or a truck with no signal, so everything it shows is inside the app. We collect nothing.

Information We Collect

None. UST Tester has no account, no login, no servers, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We never see what you look up, what you calculate, which states you work, or the forms you add.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your chosen states, your settings, and any forms you add (PDFs or images you pick from Files) are stored only on your device inside the app's own container. Deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is synced, uploaded, or backed up by us anywhere.

Sharing

When you share one of your forms, the app hands the file to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you send it, only when you tap it.

Links

Each page lists its sources, such as agency forms and manufacturer manuals. Tapping one opens that site in your browser. The app itself does not contact those sites.

Networking

The app makes network connections in one case only: to Apple's App Store and StoreKit, to offer and verify the optional UST Tester Pro purchase, which Apple handles entirely. We never receive payment details.

Children's Privacy

UST Tester does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the revised policy here.
