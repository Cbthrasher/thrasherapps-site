UST Tester Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

UST Tester fills underground storage tank test forms for the testers who do the work. It is built to work in a sump or a truck with no signal, so everything it needs is inside the app. We collect nothing.

Information We Collect

None. UST Tester has no account, no login, no servers, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We never see your sites, your customers, your readings, your forms, which states you work, or anything you look up.

Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter stays on your device inside the app's own container: your tester details, certification and test equipment, your signature, the sites and owners you save, the readings and answers for each test, the finished forms, photos you attach, your chosen states, your reminder settings, and any forms you add from Files. Deleting the app deletes them. We never sync, upload or back up any of it. Your device's own iCloud or computer backup includes the app's data in the same way it includes any app's, under your Apple account and Apple's terms.

Camera and Photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach a photo to a test, such as a water level or a gauge. Saving a finished form to Photos adds its pages to your library only when you tap Save to Photos. The app never reads your library on its own.

Reminders

Due date reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server, and you can turn them off in the app or in Settings.

Sharing

When you send a finished form, the app hands the PDF to the iOS share sheet, Mail or Messages. It goes only where you send it, only when you tap it.

Links

Each page lists its sources, such as agency forms and manufacturer manuals. Tapping one opens that site in your browser. The app itself does not contact those sites.

Networking

The app makes network connections in one case only: to Apple's App Store and StoreKit, to offer and verify the optional UST Tester Pro purchase, which Apple handles entirely. We never receive payment details.

Children's Privacy

UST Tester does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the revised policy here.
