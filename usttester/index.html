UST Tester

Fill your state's official UST tester forms at the tank. Enter the readings, see pass or fail with the rule it comes from, sign on the screen, and send the finished PDF before you leave the site. Official forms for 31 states and DC, more than 130 agency forms, and a complete test report in every other state.

The form is the job

Testers fill paper at the site, then retype it, scan it or edit a PDF at night. UST Tester fills the agency's own form from the readings you take, once.

The state's own form

Box by box on the official PDF where the state publishes one: Georgia EPD, North Carolina, Tennessee, Alabama ADEM, South Carolina, Pennsylvania DEP, Virginia DEQ, California, Kentucky, Oklahoma and more. Your name, certification, equipment and signature on every one.

Pass or fail, with the rule

The app works out the drop, the vacuum lost or the leak rate and shows the result the form's own criteria give, with the source. A missing answer is named before the form is made, so nothing goes out half filled.

Every periodic test

Spill buckets, containment and low level sumps, overfill, line leak detectors, line and tank tightness, tank gauges, sensors, cathodic protection, interstice tests, walkthroughs and more.

Sites and due dates

Every finished form files itself under the site. See what is overdue, failed or due in the next 30 days by site or by owner, with a reminder before each test comes due. Visit packets put a day's forms behind one cover page.

Send it your way

Mail, Messages or Files, or save each page to Photos for the work order. Attach photos of the readings.

Offline and private

Works in a sump with no signal. No account, no server, no tracking: your sites, readings and forms stay on your phone.

UST Tester is a field aid, not the authority. The tester decides pass or fail and signs the form. The state's current form and rules, 40 CFR 280 and the manufacturer's manual govern. Not affiliated with EPA, any state agency, NWGLDE, PEI or any manufacturer. See the support page, privacy policy and terms of use.
