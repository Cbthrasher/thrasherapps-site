What Plain Auth Does

Plain Auth stores the setup keys websites give you when you turn on two factor sign in, and shows the matching time based or counter based codes. Codes are made on your device from its clock.

Keep Your Backup Codes

Most websites give you backup or recovery codes when you turn on two factor sign in. Keep them somewhere safe outside this app. If your device is lost, reset or replaced, or an account is removed from Plain Auth, those backup codes or the website's own recovery process may be the only way back into your account. iCloud backup helps but does not replace them.

Purchases

Plain Auth is free to use and keeps up to 5 accounts. Plain Auth Pro is a single one time in app purchase, billed through your Apple Account, that adds unlimited accounts and iCloud backup and sync. It is not a subscription and never renews, and it can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Codes for accounts you have added keep working whether or not you buy Pro.
