Rack power density benchmarks

Bands

label: Traditional enterprise. value: 3 to 8 kW per rack

label: Modern environments. value: 8 to 15 kW per rack

label: AI and high performance computing. value: above 20 kW per rack and climbing

Notes

Density is climbing quickly. Size cooling and distribution for the design target, not today only.

Delta T targets

Bands

label: Modern high density target. value: about 20 to 30 F, 11 to 17 C

label: Below band. value: over cooling or airflow bypass

label: Above band. value: airflow shortfall or recirculation

Notes

An out of band delta T is a symptom. Confirm airflow and containment before adjusting setpoints.

Airflow needs by density

Bands

label: High density racks. value: 1,500 to 2,500 cfm

label: Low density racks. value: 500 to 1,000 cfm

label: Rule of thumb. value: about 120 cfm per kW

Notes

Use the rule of thumb to sanity check a calculated airflow figure.

Arc flash awareness

Bands

label: AI ready facilities. value: available fault current can exceed 100,000 A

label: Authority. value: the site arc flash study governs

Notes

This tool raises awareness only. Incident energy and boundaries come from the site arc flash study, never from an estimate.

Generator fuel notes

Bands

label: Day tank. value: about 8 hours typical

label: Refuel. value: can be refueled while running

Notes

Fuel burn rises with load. Confirm the main tank and the refueling contract for extended outages.
