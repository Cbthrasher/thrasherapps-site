Arc flash and NFPA 70E

Available fault currents in artificial intelligence ready facilities can exceed 100,000 amperes. Always work from the site arc flash study.

Lockout tagout

Control hazardous energy before servicing equipment. Critical systems are often energized by design.

Energized work reality

Critical facilities are frequently energized by design because the load cannot drop.

Emergency power off

Emergency power off exists to protect life and responders, not for routine control.

Uptime discipline

Never break redundancy without an approved procedure and awareness of the current redundancy state.
